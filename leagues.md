### Croydon & District Chess League {#croydon}

Our teams play in the league's five trophy sections: the Croydon Shield (Streatham A), Martin Cath Trophy (B), Dave Luckin Trophy (C), Deric Cox Trophy (D) and David Hodgson Trophy (R), plus a non-rated section.

The league publishes its results and tables in the ECF system: [Croydon & District Chess League page](https://lms.englishchess.org.uk/lms/organisation/1250) (this season's tables appear there once the league has set them up) &middot; [league website](https://croydonchessleague.co.uk/)

Last season's (2025/26) final tables: [Croydon Shield](https://lms.englishchess.org.uk/lms/event/9303/table) &middot; [Martin Cath Trophy](https://lms.englishchess.org.uk/lms/event/9304/table) &middot; [Dave Luckin Trophy](https://lms.englishchess.org.uk/lms/event/9305/table) &middot; [Deric Cox Trophy](https://lms.englishchess.org.uk/lms/event/9306/table) &middot; [David Hodgson Trophy](https://lms.englishchess.org.uk/lms/event/9307/table)

### Surrey County Chess Association {#surrey}

Our Surrey county matches are in the Ellery Williams Trophy (league) and the Lauder Trophy (knockout): [fixtures and results](https://www.scca.co.uk/comps/fixtures.html)

### 4NCL Online {#online}

Our online teams play in the Four Nations Chess League's online league (matches on Lichess): Streatham & Brixton A are in Division 1 and Streatham & Brixton B in Division 2 this season.
