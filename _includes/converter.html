ECF rating ↔ FIDE rating

Since FIDE changed its ratings on 1 March 2024, the English Chess Federation recommends FIDE = 0.6 × ECF + 700 up to an ECF rating of 1750, and FIDE = ECF above that (ECF note). FIDE ratings start at 1400, so results below that are only indicative.

Treat the result as an estimate: FIDE raised its ratings below 2000 in 2023–24 and the ECF chose not to follow, so the two scales no longer line up exactly (the ECF's Rating Corner on “stretch” explains why).
