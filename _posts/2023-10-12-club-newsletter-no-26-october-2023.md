*This issue welcomes in the new season OTB and Online. But we won’t forget to report what’s been going on since our last issue. All the S&B chess news that’s fit to print is here! Edited by Martin Smith. Enjoy!*

### S&BCC welcomes in the 2023-4 Season…

#### …where we have chess for you!

* **Ten teams – at all levels ─ across the three OTB leagues where we play**: nearly 500 individual games!
* **Two teams Online**, and **two in OTB knock-out competitions**. See **Our Teams** in the Appendix, for all our OTB and OL team entry details.
* **Ongoing social chess** every Tuesday afternoon at the Library.
* **Internal club events**, including a Christmas Special, Blitz and Rapid play evenings.
* **Open air chess** on Streatham Common (weather permitting!).

All that for one of the cheaper subscription rates of any London club (and it’s staying that way, for another year anyway)!

Yes, the club is thriving: we have increased our team entries, the library group continues to buzz and we are delighted to welcome a number of new members who joined over the summer: Gabriel Asman, Caelen Gopaldas, Qixiang Han, Qixuan Han, Elliot Hills, David Marshall, Charlie Pearson, Andrea Quattrone, Kazi Azizur Rahman, Ben Simpson (though the last two not playing London League for us).

### Club Calendar

Reminding you that you can keep on top of our fixtures in all the leagues, and other club events as well, via the [Calendar](https://streathamchess.org/calendar/) on our website. You’ll be lost without it!

### AGMs held during the summer, and this season’s early results:

**London League AGM 13 July:** We made a strong case for making Pimlico one of the central venues for the London League, but were not successful. On a more positive note, the AGM approved a new, clear and modern set of rules. The league has a new website, and will be using the LMS (i.e. the [ECF League Management System](https://lms.englishchess.org.uk/lms/node/2)) for fixtures, match results, tables etc. It has introduced a Women’s Division. (James Toon – *who is on the League Committee [Ed]*)

**London League Division 1:** We have a strong squad and should be competitive against almost all teams, aiming to finish in the top half of a very strong division. A welcome return is the Eastman Cup competition where we begin with a home match vs Mushrooms in November. (James Toon).

{% include image.html url="/assets/image_uploads/nl26-st-margarets-hall.jpg" description="Playing at St Margaret’s Hall, Bethnal Green" %}

**London League Division 2:** Division 2 will be competitive, mostly against other clubs’ 1st teams, so there will be strong opposition, especially on the top boards, but it will tail off lower down. We will play at St Margaret’s Hall, Bethnal Green (*see right*), and other clubs’ home venues. Unfortunately, for reasons beyond our control, our first two fixtures have been postponed, and we have to wait for the New Year to start our season. (Antony Hall) *[Welcome back Antony, after a couple of years off. Ed]*

**Central London League AGM:** There is no report of a CLL AGM, because this League doesn’t have one. This lack of transparency and accountability in the league’s management is a frequent source of frustration. Even so, it remains popular and participation is high. Matches are almost exclusively played at Churchill Gardens Community Centre, Pimlico – which has proved satisfactory for all. (Martin Smith)

**Central London League, Division 2:** We narrowly avoided relegation from Division 2 last season but have now strengthened the squad, so that will be an unwelcome surprise for our opponents. S&BCC1 will be probably the highest-rated team on average and we are aiming for promotion to Division 1. Our campaign had a solid start (a 2.5-all draw) on 21 September against a decent Hammersmith team – one of the better in the Division. Very satisfactory. (James Toon)

**Central London League, Division 4:** S&BCC2’s season kicked off on September 14 with a hard fought 2-all draw against Wanstead 2. Special mention to Elliot Hills who won his first competitive OTB standard play game. (Paul Ritchie)

**4NCL Online, Season 8:** There have been three rounds to date. Our A team is top of Group B in Division 1 after two wins and a draw. Streatham B, now in Division 3, made a slow start losing its first two matches but got its mojo back with a win in the Round 3. Thanks to Matt Tillett for managing the two teams. There are places available in our 4NCL Online squad – just let Matt know if you would like to play in any of the remaining matches (contact details are in the PDF newsletter).

**Surrey League AGM 25 June:** Surrey has adopted Fischer timing, at G75 + 10s (though QP guillotine finish will be permitted by player agreement). The playing session will now be a mandatory 3 hours to accommodate the possible longer games. The League will explore how to involve more women in its activities. A reminder, by the way, that S&BCC members are eligible to play in the County sides (Martin Smith)

{% include image.html align="left" url="/assets/image_uploads/nl26-alexander-cup-kingston.jpg" description="Ben front left, with Matt Tillett and James Toon beyond (The Thin Blue Line!)" %}

**Alexander Cup Knock-out:** this is the only SCCA competition in which we currently play. There are seven teams battling it out this year: we were drawn against strong Kingston CC in round 1, and went down fighting 3 v 7 on October 3rd. A noteworthy win was played by new member Ben Simpson, who (with white in a QGD Exchange) played a nice positional exchange sac to beat his higher rated opponent Will Taylor (see [https://lichess.org/study/PWSSXrKr](https://lichess.org/study/PWSSXrKr/ccr6wIxK)).

{% include lichess_pgn.html study="PWSSXrKr" chapter="ccr6wIxK" %}

Mark O’Neil was our other winner with a sparkling win as Black. He too was 100 rating points behind his opponent, Julian Way. Full score card on the SCCA website [here](https://www.scca.co.uk/comps/comps_index.html).

**Croydon and District Chess League AGM:** Last season the CDCL successfully trialled default incremental timing, also at G75 + 10spm, but with a guillotine finish if agreed by players and captains. At this year’s AGM some minor tweaking to the rules was agreed. The default start time remains 7.40pm. The CDCL starts in mid-October so no match results yet.

#### …and, not forgetting the Club’s own AGM on Zoom, 5 August.

No yawning at the back please: we have to hold an AGM so you can keep tabs on The Management, viz: Martin Smith, President; Richard Tillett, Secretary; Paul Ritchie, Treasurer: and James Toon – who were the only ones at the meeting apart from Matt Hannay (thanks Matt!). Naturally The Management re-elected itself 🙂.

The President and Secretary noted a highly satisfactory season in the Leagues and at the Library Group, and announced this year’s Player of the Year (see next item). The club accounts showed that we broke even during the 2022-23 season, and it was agreed to hold member subscriptions at the current level for 2023-24 (full £45; concessionary £22.50; game fee £4.50): to be reviewed at the 2024 AGM. Teams and League entries for the 2023-24 season were agreed, though the Croydon League entry had still to be determined (but is now done; see all in the table in the Appendix).

A programme of internal club events was sketched out. We also identified the need to smarten up the Club’s policies with respect to data protection (members should be asked to positively sign up to our Privacy Policy), and the safe-guarding of juniors and vulnerable adults. We agreed to make another appeal (this is it!) for volunteers to help do some of the jobs to maintain and develop the club. Martin Smith announced that with regret he would have to step back from formal club responsibilities at the 2024 AGM, due to domestic responsibilities.

### Player of the Year

The annual Player of the Year (POTY) award has been running since 2008/9. The POTY is a member who has done sterling service in the season for the club: in the front line at the board (OTB and/or online), and possibly behind the scenes as a captain or organiser. The club management team consults team captains before making the award at the AGM.

{% include image.html url="/assets/image_uploads/nl26-andrew-stone.jpg" description="Andrew Stone" %}

We are delighted to announce that **for the 2022-3 season** the award goes to First Team regular **Andrew Stone**. Andrew performed with flying colours in our top league teams OTB and online, and headed our Major team in the SCCU London Club Rapid. He has always responded enthusiastically to our team selection requests.

{% include image.html align="left" url="/assets/image_uploads/nl26-poty-shield.jpg" description="The Player of the Year shield, with Andrew Stone’s name inscribed for 2022/23" %}

Congratulations Andrew! We have not been able present the trophy yet, but Andrew’s name is already inscribed on it.

Actually, Andrew has ancient history chess-wise: if you’d like to find out more (and fancy another visit to the [Streatham and Brixton Chess Blog](http://streathambrixtonchess.blogspot.com/) back catalogue) click [here](http://streathambrixtonchess.blogspot.com/2014/12/county-counting-5-in-jellie-mould.html).

### Chess on the Common

{% include image.html align="left" url="/assets/image_uploads/nl26-streatham-common.jpg" description="Chess on Streatham Common: the Grand Opening event in May 2023" %}

It started during our 150th Anniversary year 2021 when we took a stall at the Rookery Market during the Streatham Festival. The Friends of Streatham Common then installed three permanent chess tables near the Rookery Café – and we helped them with a Grand Opening event in May this year. We were out in force again on 19 August. Our friends from the sadly demised Crystal Palace CC donated four sets to be loaned out from the Café – and we know they’ve been used because they’ve been all mixed up! The Ghosts of Crystal Palace (Graham Keane, Chris Bernard, Mark O’Neil, Martin Dale – all now S&BCC members), and a few others, met up on a couple of dry and sunny September afternoons for some reunion blitz; and that’s worth repeating next summer when the weather returns. You can keep in touch with other Common Chessers via its WhatsApp group (message me to join it; contact details are in the PDF newsletter).

**Our next Streatham Common gig will be on Saturday 21 October** at this year’s Streatham Festival, when S&BCC will be strutting its stuff again from 11.00am to 3.00pm (see our bit in the [Festival Programme](https://streathamfestival.com/)). See you there!

Help setting up from 10.30am will be very welcome!

### False Flag Operation?

In addition to securing the early-season S&BCC team results reported above, Streatham players have been out in force in the UNATS team in the London Public Service Chess league (once the Civil Service League). UNATS represents players ‘unattached’ to any other Civil Service Departmental team; it is run by S&B member Martin Cowley, and has become almost an S&B outfit under another name. In its first match this season (28 September), our guys played 4 of its 7 boards: James Toon, John Bennett, Robin Haldane, and Jagdip Patel all winning their games in an overall 5-2 victory over Railsport 1. James’s interesting tussle is [here](https://lichess.org/3OrJi4vd).
